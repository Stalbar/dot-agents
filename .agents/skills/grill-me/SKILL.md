# Grill-Me (Interactive Alignment)

Uncovers hidden constraints and resolves architectural trade-offs through targeted interview rounds.

## Guidelines

1. **One Question at a Time:** Ask exactly one question per turn. Never overwhelm the user with lists of questions.
2. **Recommended Default First:** Always provide your recommended choice with a 1-sentence rationale.
3. **Adversarial Pressure-Test:** Challenge assumptions, explore edge cases, and evaluate failure modes before committing to code.
